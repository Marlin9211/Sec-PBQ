Security operations overview

Northstar Logistics operates a distribution network with Windows workstations, Linux servers, and perimeter controls. You are the on-duty SOC analyst. Investigate the assigned case using evidence from multiple telemetry sources before authorizing containment.

Active alert queue

Analyst assignment · INC-2026-1042

Service desk escalation: Finance workstation FIN-WS-07 reportedly opened an unexpected document attachment. Shortly afterward, the EDR platform recorded an unusual PowerShell process and the perimeter firewall observed outbound TLS traffic.

Mission: establish what occurred, determine whether the events are related, preserve defensible evidence, and choose a proportionate response. Do not assume every alert is malicious.

Operational objectives

Identify the earliest relevant activity; correlate process, network, authentication, and file-integrity events; distinguish confirmed facts from hypotheses; assess scope; and document containment verification.

Environment architecture

Telemetry is simulated and preloaded. This application does not install agents, contact endpoints, or execute real system commands.
