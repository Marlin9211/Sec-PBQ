Linux Incident Investigation PBQ

You are a Tier 1 SOC analyst. Investigate a suspicious outbound connection, identify the responsible process, contain it, and verify that the connection is gone. This is an original training exercise—not an actual CompTIA exam item.

Incident Brief

The SIEM generated an alert for FIN-WS-07, a Linux workstation used by the Finance team. Threat intelligence identifies one external IP as associated with command-and-control activity.

Known malicious IP
198.51.100.42

Your objective
Find the connection → identify its PID → inspect the process → inspect its open files → terminate it → verify containment.

Investigation Progress

1. Discover available commands
2. Identify suspicious connection and PID
3. Identify the process
4. Inspect files used by the process
5. Terminate malicious process
6. Verify containment
